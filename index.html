Specialized Engineering

Magnetic and electromagnetic engineering

Beyond telecom operations, our division supports manufacturers, research institutions, and broadcasters with specialized RF solutions.

MSW Filters

Magnetostatic wave filters for sharp frequency selectivity in microwave equipment.

Ferrite-Based Antennas

Ferrite rod and loopstick antenna support for radio, AM broadcast, and RFID.

Magnetic Materials

Evaluating how magnetic materials shape antenna impedance, resonance, and coupling.

EMC and Interference

Finding interference sources and recommending fixes that keep equipment reliable.

Research and development: magnetic materials, microwave components, and ferrite technology for what comes next.
